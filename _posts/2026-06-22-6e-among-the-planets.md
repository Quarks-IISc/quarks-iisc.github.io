Sometimes, the best shots are the ones you can't plan for. I originally intended to capture a simple, quiet photo of the solar system at work: a vertical alignment of Venus, the Moon, and Jupiter. After running to my room to grab my camera, I returned to find an aeroplane entering the frame the second I got back to the roof. With no time to adjust settings, I shot it as is. Thanks to Flightradar, I later identified the plane as flight 6E7586, forever frozen in a lucky transit through the cosmos.
