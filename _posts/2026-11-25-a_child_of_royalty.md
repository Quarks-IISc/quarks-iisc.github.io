*I crave my existence.* <br>
I’m not audible to anyone <br>
While I’m in the show, <br>
But as soon as I turn off <br>
It’s a wildfire through their doors. <br>
I’m a precocious child <br>
A lamb to a royal high <br>
they can afford my expenses <br>
but not the freedom of living by my virtue. <br><br>


I own a graveyard of dreams <br>
None of which cost a penny, <br>
yet they expect my address to be <br>
in the sweetest tone known to liberty. <br>
No excuse is granted <br>
They’ve elaborated my presence, <br>
And as I glance down the balcony <br>
I’m about to throw up. <br>
All the honey that slipped off my tongue <br>
was actually disguised blood, <br>
These masses don’t know- <br>
I’m an aged victim of their monarch.
