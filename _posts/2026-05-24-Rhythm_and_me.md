<div style="text-align: center;">

Squeezing my way through the hurdle<br>
listening to someone else's rhythm<br>
I wove a part for myself<br>
Thinking I knew my rhythm<br><br>

Playing with the melody<br>
Strings got strained<br>
But I kept plucking them<br>
Thinking I knew my rhythm<br><br>

Almost halfway through<br>
I tried tuning harmony<br>
But borrowed strings could not withstand<br>
A new rhythm had to be found<br><br>

Performance at a halt<br>
For I shall find my own rhythm<br>
New strings were weaving<br>
A new rhythm forging its way<br><br>

Savoring the melody<br>
Harmony in its bliss<br>
I rejoiced and danced<br>
My rhythm was singing along<br>

</div>
