<div style="text-align: center;">

I saw a couple walking together around a serene<br>
Reminded me of a movie scene<br>
Singing opera the birds flying around<br>
The wind blowing the flute till i was found<br>
Sitting on the chair I hear leaves rustling<br>
No sound of music, only cars bustling<br><br>

Oh did I not hear a single note<br>
To hear that sound I put on a tape<br>
And guess what? It changed my fate<br>
Do not play hero if you hear violins<br>
No need to cry if you hear the harp<br>
Turn off the music, wait and see<br>
Life is what you hear it to be<br><br>

Quarrels and fights have sharp notes<br>
Fairytails and lights possess sweet tones<br>
Ah yes I hear it while walking together<br>
You have changed the background of my life forever<br>
Faraway still I hear the song you sing<br>
A song I kept in myself within<br><br>

I tried on headphones in my free time<br>
just to find, you were the only sound in my mind<br>
When I saw the bus you rode go away<br>
All I could hear was it driving away<br>
Will I be able to hear myself then<br>
When I hear you sing that song again<br><br>

Life has no background music,<br>
Will you press play for me, again?

</div>
