I’m passing by, and I meet your eyes <br>
No, this is not a love story, oh my\! <br>
Your vacant eyes nearly brush away soon <br>
Before I smile, and you’re left confused <br><br>

I light up like I’ve lived my days, <br>
And I’m satisfied with the world I’ve made <br>
Like I’m surrounded by floating lights and stars, <br>
And you and I, we’ve come so far<br><br>

I light up like we’re in a place<br>
Of happy souls and beaming rays<br>
Like the sun’s come out from a hidden cage<br>
And it’s shining on us, just you and me<br><br>

While I don’t know you, <br>
I smile wide,<br>
And I light up for<br>
No reason at all<br>
And you look at me, <br>
And you smile;<br>
Now look how you’ve lit up,<br>
For no reason at all
