This painting honours a rare, surviving art form: the making of Maa Durga idols during Navratri in the Bundelkhandi style. One of the oldest idol-making traditions of Central India, it uses only soil to shape the idols. The style has almost vanished everywhere else, but in Jabalpur (Madhya Pradesh) it is preserved and celebrated.

It is a highlight of the grand festival celebrations of Sanskardhani Jabalpur. These idols are considered among the heaviest ever carried on shoulders in procession. Every year, two of the most renowned idols are adorned with crores’ worth of gold, silver, diamonds and other precious stones.

This artwork depicts **Maa Nunhai**, who is adorned mostly in silver. Her counterpart, **Maa Sunarhai**, is adorned mostly in gold.
