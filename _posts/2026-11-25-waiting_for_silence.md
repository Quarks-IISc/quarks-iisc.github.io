It feels as if silence speaks to me, <br>
like it knows my name. <br><br>

It tries to pull me under, <br>
wrap around my thoughts, <br>
hold me still under its weight. <br>
But I run <br>
into crowded rooms, <br>
into laughter that isn’t mine, <br>
into noise loud enough <br>
to drown the one within me. <br><br>

And for a while, <br>
it works. <br>
Until the echoes return. <br><br>

So I wonder, <br>
do I need to be alone? <br>
Or does silence need me <br>
to sit with it, <br>
until I can't remember <br>
what noise felt like? <br><br>

Perhaps, silence is alone, <br>
and I am simply <br>
what it takes <br>
to not be? <br><br>

Maybe it isn’t patient. <br>
Maybe it’s waiting me out. <br>
Because no matter how far I run, <br>
I end up right here <br>
with it.
