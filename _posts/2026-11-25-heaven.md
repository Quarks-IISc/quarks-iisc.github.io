He offered a trade of a quiet corner, <br>
to close the books, stop the ambition, <br>
buy a cheap seat in heaven by curling to a ball. <br><br>

For three hours (seconds), the room turned to glass, <br>
terrified that the hands that carried me <br>
were secretly tired from the weight. <br><br>

My mother opened the door, <br>
and she looked up at their heaven <br>
and told them to burn in hell.
