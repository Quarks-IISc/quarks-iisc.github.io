With her head bowed mournfully to her heart, <br>
a woman stood in the crevasse of her balcony watching<br>  
the flood of people in the streets.  <br>
Framed by strands of coarse, bleached hair,<br>  
the colors they brought with them pushed her  <br>
further into her mind.  <br>
She could not follow any one of them.<br><br>  
   
Amongst them,  <br>
a face that strayed<br>   
from the masses  <br>
and into stillness,  <br>
   
Turned up like a child looking at their grown-ups.<br>  
Presumably with their lips slightly parted,  <br>
and their eyes  <br>
lingering.  <br><br>
   
Of course neither of them could ever know  <br>
if their eyes had met,  <br>
And the stranger could not have known <br>  
that they had been perceived,  <br>
but she felt as if  <br>
they had reached out  <br>
and taken her with the softness of their hand.<br><br>
