<div style="text-align: center;">

Saw a fleeting dream at night,  <br>
Of a flower in bloom,  <br>
Delicate and pretty, yet pompous and frail, <br> 
Dancing with the wind and talking to the rain,  <br>
A proud performer and a shy charmer,  <br>
Of the flower in bloom, this is a tale.<br><br>

It boasted of life, its petals do never fade,  <br>
Nor did its leaves ever wilt under sun or hail,  <br>
Carefree and joyous, a smile reincarnate,  <br>
So it brought near many suitors, but in love it did fail,<br>  
Of a naive flower in bloom, this is a tale.<br><br>

It loved most where it found love,  <br>
Even the pollen and the dirt which adorned its skirt,<br>  
But wanted it more, so fell prey to a flirt,  <br>
Alas\! if only she knew the price,  <br>
Alas\! if only she didn't folly thrice, <br> 
But of a blind flower in bloom, this is a tale.<br><br>

For a hand which adored her, she gave her love,  <br>
But in mad obsession it did break her stalk,  <br>
Broken and violated by her very first love,  <br>
But the foolish flower didn't lose it all,  <br>
Hoped another would come her way,  <br>
Heal her, caress her, make her whole again,<br>  
But a folly repeat she made in her choice,  <br>
Of this broken flower, this is a tale.<br><br>

For this new suitor was a master of words,  <br>
Spoke what it wished to hear, and cried when it meant to tear,<br>  
It poured itself into him, every once and every grain,  <br>
Only to get abandoned when it most needed him,  <br>
Got played again\! you silly flower, but lesson you didn't learn,<br>  
Of this foolish flower, this is a tale.<br><br>

The third was no better either, even I now feel remorse,  <br>
He gave it hope again, maybe all worse was gone,  <br>
But a thief he turned out and stole what it loved most,<br>  
For a petal he did pluck, and then he was gone,  <br>
Broken, betrayed and empty, with all its charms lost,<br>  
Of this wilting flower, this is a tale.<br><br>

Oh\! How you pain me, you pathetic beauty,  <br>
If only I could but lend you my hand,  <br>
But forced to watch bare, cruel my fate is,<br>  
And cruel your life, your emotions and care,  <br>
But tell me the answer, of just one question I have, <br> 
If a true love does come in your way after all,  <br>
Will you but judge him right and do him trust,  <br>
Or will he pay the price of the three before,  <br>
Or compete with what the three did right?  <br>
But alas\! Alas\! For my dream does fade,  <br>
And your right to answer it did just take,  <br>
A right I did steal and it haunts me to this day, <br> 
Of a lost flower, this was a tale...
