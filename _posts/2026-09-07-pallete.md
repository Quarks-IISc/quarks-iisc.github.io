A silent year old, yet I stare, <br>
With the mystery I still bear;<br>
Of all the strokes I brushed,<br>
Which made you go hushed?<br>
For my eyes hear no any hue,<br>
So our paints halt into a due.<br>
A silent year old yet I stare,<br>
At the palette then you were.<br>
For yet invisible you blend.<br>
So a painting we lose, friend.<br>
