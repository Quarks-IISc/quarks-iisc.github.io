A lazy walk through a hazy morn' <br>
greets one with thriving flush just born<br>
alongside the flowers having the beauty worn<br><br>

A sprightly stroll through a brightly noon<br>
under the clear sky of blessed boon<br>
which awaits the entry of mighty moon<br><br>

And a hastened pace through darkened dusk<br>
when the wind blows to rise the husk<br>
as the heaven hides the sun under its tusk<br><br>

Shall they not be enow hither<br>
to allow one's life to tether<br>
lest their life begins to wither<br>
