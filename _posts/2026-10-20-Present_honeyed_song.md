“Present, present, what art thou?” <br><br>

Drops of honey upon the heart,<br>
drawn by seconds that buzz and dart.<br>
Poured without pause into a dim jar,<br>
Thus stands the past, while the futures’ far.<br><br>

Yet often a hint of sour is there,<br>
Of half-hope and utter despair.<br>
In merrier times, will I still care,<br>
If you weren’t always sweet and fair?<br><br>

Yet someday, somewhere over trodden ways,<br>
I’m caught saying: “Those were the days\!”<br>
Shut my eyes, and enter a dream,<br>
Of these days that now bitter seem.<br><br>

The tale of today’s a dream of morrow,<br>
My delight with a tinge of sorrow\!<br>
Enlightened thus, what joy it brings\!<br>
The present’s this honeyed song I sing.<br><br>

“Present, present what art thou?”<br>
A present in a crystal orb art thee,<br>
One I will cherish to eternity.
