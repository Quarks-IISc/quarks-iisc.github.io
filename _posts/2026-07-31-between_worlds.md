I stand at the edge  <br>
one foot in what was,   <br>
one in what won’t remember me.<br><br>

“You don’t belong here,”  <br>
the new world says,  <br>
wrapping itself around   <br>
my name.<br><br>

“But you don’t exist anymore,” <br> 
the other replies.<br>  
Steady.  <br>
Unshaken.<br><br>

I look between them,<br>  
same hands,  <br>
different pulse.<br><br>

One aches to return,  <br>
to fit into memories  <br>
that still call me home.<br><br>

The other,  <br>
quiet, unfamiliar, <br> 
doesn’t ask permission  <br> 
to live.<br><br>

“Choose,” they both say.<br>  
And I realize  <br>
only one of them  <br>
is letting me breathe.<br>
