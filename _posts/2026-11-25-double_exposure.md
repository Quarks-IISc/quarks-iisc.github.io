The glass cold against my forehead,  <br>
shivering whenever a bass drum rolls down the street. <br><br>

Whistle, the smell of sulfur and slap of palms on the drums  <br>
A tangle of cheap yellow fairy lights leaking over the balcony railings.<br>  
Nobody on that tarmac is calculating the weight of an hour.<br><br>

Look closely at the window<br><br>

3:00 AM margins.  <br>
The dull buzz of the tube light chewing through the quiet.<br>  
Just the hum of the fan, white glare on lined paper  <br>
And a mug with a dried brown ring at the bottom.<br><br>

September’s got twenty-four reasons to throw sugar into the street,<br>  
plus one date that’s supposed to mean something,  <br>
except it’s just the Thursday between two hall tickets.<br><br>

Look closely at the window<br><br>

it reflects the lamp behind me  <br>
and lets the firework burst into my reflection. <br> 
A red flare blooming in the dark,  <br>
trapped in a double exposure.<br><br>

Cold glass.  <br>
Warm street.  <br>
I kill the switch.<br>  
Let the city sing without me.<br>
