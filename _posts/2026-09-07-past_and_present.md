| Past | Present |
| :--- | :--- |
| The screen blurred in front of me<br>Tears spill on the worksheet<br>It's 3 in the morning and still<br>I have 15 more questions and<br>15 more days<br>For an exam which<br>Everybody says<br>Will decide my fate<br>‘Forever?’ I ask<br>Because the climb feels hard<br>Is this what it takes to reach the peak?<br>What will be of me?<br>Where will I stand?<br>Or more specifically…<br>Where will my picture be,<br>In those big banners outside<br>Every day as I inch closer<br>I wonder, I wish<br>I could meet Future me<br>Ask her, “Will it be worth it?”<br>Will she hug me in joy<br>And pat me, saying “Yes! It was!”<br>Or would she still look at me<br>And ask back, “Was this the right choice?”<br>“I still have to keep running?”<br>I wish she could hold me through this<br>Because the climb feels hard<br>But she would be standing at the peak<br>Won’t she? | The screen keeps loading<br>For what feels like an eternity<br>I sit with my eyes shut tight<br>And prayer on my lips<br>Peeked at the screen<br>And called out to them<br>Do I celebrate or do I worry?<br>Is this good?<br>No, is this *good enough*?<br>10 calls, 12 tabs and 3 conversations later<br>I get to know<br>Maybe it might be good enough?<br>Good enough to be on the banners<br>To get a respectable seat<br>To make all our sacrifices worth it<br>Lists of choices and preferences<br>And finally that wonderful mail<br>That finalizes our choice<br>I know now where I will be<br>And I wish<br>I could hold my past self<br>And tell her, “Yes! It was!”<br>I would look at her<br>And say, “It was the right choice”<br>“And I'll keep running!”<br>Because even though the climb feels hard<br>There are many peaks we’ve got<br>To keep climbing up and up<br>The sky’s never the limit |
