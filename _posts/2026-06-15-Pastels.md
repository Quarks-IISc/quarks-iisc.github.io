<div style="text-align: center;">

Excruciating, how soft shades of nostalgia can be,  <br>
The dull beige of the wrapping paper that encircles  <br>
the sprigs of lavender,  <br>
The tawny fan, whirring ever so slightly <br>  
drying them out,  <br>
The pale strawberry of the tulip vase, where they eventually found home,  <br>
The sunlight-coloured corner of our drawing-room as  <br>
I made my peace with leaving,  <br>
The mint green walls of my room where I exchanged wistful  <br>
smiles and cracked nonchalant jokes,  <br>
The azure lace of my mom’s dress as she shook  <br>
her head completely aware,  <br>
That my caramel-coloured coffee and wit-laced comments <br>  
remained my primary currencies of affection,  <br>
The teal of my best friend’s car as she pulled  <br>
up for one last farewell,  <br>
The starburst of mauve cracks on the porch as <br>  
I choked back sobs and feigned composure,  <br>
The cool grey of the pavement as we hurtled  <br>
towards the airport,   <br>
The buttercup yellow biscuits, as I peppered <br> 
the drive’s conversation with quips and puns,  <br>
And the albatross white of my college-bound  <br>
plane where I heart-brokenly realised,  <br>
That I had found solace in snark for too long, <br> 
And been beaten to the punchline. <br>
