<div style="text-align: center;">

The world’s a carousel,  <br>
Whirling, bustling, and merry,<br>  
Colorful are its folks,  <br>
Colorful are its scenes,  <br>
Colorful are its lights,   <br>
A brilliant blur becomes life. <br> 
Round, around, around and round,  <br>
I spin, I go, and keep on going,   <br>
To which the kid inside claps,  <br>
Giggles and laughs,  <br>
Excited, elated. <br><br>

No end knows the roundabout.<br>   
So spinning still, I start to wonder:<br>  
How sweet if I may keep,  <br>
Circling around with the world’s riches-<br>  
Let it fill me, a pailful of memory,  <br>
Golden sands of untold stories,  <br>
A song of myself, and mine to sing,  <br> 
Lingering on my smiling lips,  <br>
Forever and forever\!<br><br>

And thus, a jolly kid on a dizzying carousel,<br>  
Unwilling to leave, wild with glee.  <br>
A year well spent in these lights I need,<br>  
Then home is where I want to be.<br><br>

My home’s a sculpture,  <br>
Silent, solid, calm, and serene, <br> 
with a heart that has a me-shaped hole.<br>  
Tired of the lights, tired of the riches,  <br>
I return home a sleepy kid,   <br>
With my pailful of memory, <br>
Golden sands of well-told stories,<br>  
A song of myself, a verse well sung.  <br> 
With heavy eyes, into the hole I’ll climb,<br>  
And softly close the door behind. <br><br>

The walls are dull, and the smells are known,<br>  
Yet what a grace to be at home\!  <br>
Where every nook and cranny’s my kin, <br> 
Where childhood steps still echo soft,  <br>
Where pleasant chimes the falling rain,  <br>
Where calming is the morning breeze,  <br>
Wrapped in blankets, I am at ease.  <br>
Silently mothering the kid inside,   <br>
The me-shaped hole makes me complete. 

When peace, not pleasure, my heart craves,   
Leaving behind the roundabout,  
Homeward turn my tiny steps.  
Deep in my soul this truth I keep:  
There’s a me-shaped hole where I’m lulled to sleep.
