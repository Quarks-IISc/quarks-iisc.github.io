Nothing secret ships to the browser: the endpoint is an address, not a credential. The worst anyone can do with it is send us mail, which they could do from their own client anyway. Why a classic POST and not fetch(): attachments. FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint is documented for JSON only, so files go through the normal form submission and `_next` bounces the visitor to /post-office/thanks/. To swap providers later, change `form action` and the four `_`-prefixed hidden inputs. Everything else on the page is provider-agnostic. {% endcomment %}

About staying anonymous

Only the name of the piece and the file itself are required. Leave the rest blank and your submission reaches us with nothing attached to it but the work. If you do give us a name, we will publish it alongside your piece.

Notice!

If your piece contains inappropriate content which we can't publish or we face other issues, we will write to you and ask. If you posted anonymously or the contact details are wrong, then we may not publish the content.
