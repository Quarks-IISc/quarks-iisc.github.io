🦆 Duck Duck Loot

Quarks Quacks Treasure Hunt

Solve clues, scan QR codes, and race your team to the top. The hunt spans the entire IISc campus!

How It Works

  • Follow the Main Quest — a chain of clues unique to your team. Solve each to unlock the next.
  • Attempt Side Quests — one active at a time, shared across all teams. First to solve wins!
  • Some clues need a QR scan, others a text answer. Read carefully.
  • Side Quest points add to your team's Quarks Quacks leaderboard score!
